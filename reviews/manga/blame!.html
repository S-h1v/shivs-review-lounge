BLAME!

A review by Shiv

This is a manga that has one of my favorite world. A complete mechanical desert of buildings and machines.

Story

There isn't much when you think about it, but it is good nonetheless. Actually, it's really good. Not a masterpiece, but definitely on the better side of stories that you can find in the medium.

Characters

The character designs can be called ugly, I guess, but it goes grow in you like other manga art styles do. The creature design on the other hand is probably some of the best I've ever seen. Some of the most creative uses of mechanical parts to make humanoids has been done here to fully immerse you in this massive world that's put in front of you. While there not being much dialogue, I doesnt need it to tell us the story that it's showing us. This leads us to Kyrii/Killy out MC, the lack of a lot of dailouge also gives him the traits of some of my favorite characters - doesn't talk much or show emotion much but is a complete badass, a bit stoic in a way. The side cast is pretty small, but that doesn't detract their role in the story, because the ones that are important to the story, are pretty well handled I'd say.

Art

The settling for the story is more of a niche in fantasy as a whole. The entire deserted cyberpunk aesthetic is not done that often even looking outside manga for a second, but this is exactly what I crave for and Blame! delivers and exceeds what you want from it through its sheer beauty of the large scale shots gives us of the place that out protagonist is. Places so large that it makes us feel utterly insignificant in the grander scale of things.

Final thoughts

I absolutely adore the world in this manga, and on a reread, where I actually paid attention to the story, I've gained a higher appreciation for the manga. This is absolutely one of my favorite mangas now and one that I can recommend to anyone who is interested into deserted cyberpunk at all.

I highly recommend it to anyone why wants to get into more niche series.

Overall: 9.5/10
