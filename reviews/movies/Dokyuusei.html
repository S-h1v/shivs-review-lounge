Dandadan S2

A review by Shiv

This is truly the prettiest a yaoi has ever been, and sadly the prettiest it'll ever be. A simple story of two guys falling for each other and slipping down their hole of love as time goes on. I liked what we got from it. I'm kinda sad that it's only one hour, cause that's the the only thing stopping from liking this more. I just need more time with these guys, and while the side characters aren't that important in this story, I wish we got a more interactions with people other than the main couple. Talking about art, It's all stellar. The character designs are ridiculously pretty, the water colour look in art direction gels with it perfectly, and this being a movie gives us incredibly smooth animation. The voice actors also put in an incredible shift for this movie. My mind would be blown if I see another yaoi get a treatment like this If you're into yaoi, or want to dive into the genre, this is easily the strongest recommendation I can give you, But even with that

I just wanted more of it.

Overall: 7.5/10
